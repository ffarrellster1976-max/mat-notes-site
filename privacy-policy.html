Mat Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Mat Notes is a jiu-jitsu training app published by Frank Farrell. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Mat Notes has no accounts and no analytics or advertising code. What you enter stays on your device. We do not receive it and have no copy of it.

Information you enter

The app stores these on your device only:

  • Your profile: belt, stripes, training preferences, goal, and whether you are 40 or older
  • Training logs, including effort ratings and your notes
  • Daily check-ins: sleep, energy, soreness, and whether you reported new or sharp pain
  • Technique progress
  • Competitions you add and any Smoothcomp profile link you save

Some of this, such as check-ins, may be sensitive to you. It is not sent to us.

Third-party services

Parts of the app load content from other companies. When that happens, your device connects to them directly, and they can receive technical information such as your IP address and device or browser details. Their own privacy policies apply.

  • Google Fonts supplies the app's fonts.
  • YouTube supplies video thumbnails and, when you press play, the videos. Playback uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Wikimedia Commons supplies technique photos.

The app also has links that open outside it, including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Smoothcomp and the IBJJF. Those sites apply their own policies once you open them.

Backups you share

If you export a backup, the app creates a file containing your data and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice, and it is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. To delete it, use Learn, then Settings, then Erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes it, although your device's own backup service may keep a copy under your device settings.

Children

Mat Notes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information

Mat Notes gives general training information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
