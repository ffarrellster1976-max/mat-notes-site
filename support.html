Mat Notes Support

Mat Notes is a jiu-jitsu training app with techniques for every belt, a daily focus, a training log, strength work and body care.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, email support@personallifeassistant.org. Please include your device model and which version of the app you are using.

Do I need an account?

No. Mat Notes works without an account, and everything you enter stays on your device.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Learn, then Settings, and choose Export backup. Save or send the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, install Mat Notes, go to the same place and choose Import backup.

How do I delete my data?

Open Learn, then Settings, and choose Erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Videos or photos will not load

Technique videos and photos come from the internet, so check your connection. Everything else works offline.
